# Privacy Policy: Send Celebration for Gmail

Last updated: 2026-10-13

Send Celebration for Gmail ("the extension") shows a short celebration effect when you send an email in Gmail on the web. This policy explains what the extension does with data.

## Summary
The extension does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal data. It makes no network requests.

## What the extension accesses
- **Gmail pages (mail.google.com).** The extension runs on Gmail only to notice when you press Send. It watches the Send button and whether the compose window closes. It does **not** read, store or transmit the content, subject, recipients or attachments of your emails, your inbox, or your contacts.

## What the extension stores
- **Settings** (theme, sound, volume, quiet hours, daily goal, on/off) are saved with Chrome's `storage.sync`. Chrome may sync these between your own browsers if you are signed into Chrome with sync enabled. This is handled by your browser; the extension's developer never receives it.
- **Game stats** (score, level, send counts, combo, badges, last message shown) are saved with Chrome's `storage.local` on your device only. They contain no email content.

You can delete the stats at any time with "Reset stats" on the options page, or by removing the extension.

## Network, analytics and third parties
- No analytics, advertising, tracking or error reporting.
- No remote code. All code, the font and the sounds (generated on your device) are included in the extension package.
- No data is shared with third parties.

## Permissions
- `storage`: save your settings and stats as described above.
- Access to `https://mail.google.com/*`: required to detect when you send an email.

## Children
The extension is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.

## Changes
If this policy changes, the date above will be updated and the changes will be described in the extension's release notes.
